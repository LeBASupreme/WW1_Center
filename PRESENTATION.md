# WW1 Remembrance Centre — Project Overview

## The Website

A full website for the WW1 Remembrance Centre in Portsmouth, built from scratch.

---

## Public Pages

| Page | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Home** | The centrepiece of the website. As visitors scroll down, they are taken on a virtual walkthrough of the museum — moving through each room as if they were physically there. It creates an immersive first impression before they even step through the door. |
| **Shop** | A fully functional online shop where visitors can browse and purchase souvenirs from the centre. Each product has its own page with a photo, description and price. Customers pay securely online and receive their order by post. |
| **News** | A dedicated section for articles, announcements and updates from the centre. Each article has its own page with a title, text and photo. New articles can be published at any time from the admin panel. |
| **Battlefield Trips** | A listings page for all organised tours to the Western Front and other WW1 sites. Each trip has its own detail page with a description, date and capacity information. |
| **Donate** | A simple donation page where supporters can contribute to the centre. They can choose from preset amounts (£5, £10, £25, £50) or enter any amount they wish. The process takes under a minute and is fully secure. |
| **Volunteer** | A sign-up form for people who wish to volunteer at the centre. Applicants fill in their name, contact details and availability. Their application goes straight to the admin panel for review. |
| **About** | A page presenting the history and mission of the WW1 Remembrance Centre — who you are, what you do, and why it matters. |
| **Contact** | A page with the centre's contact information so visitors, schools and press can easily get in touch. |

---

## Online Payments (Stripe)

All payments on the site go through **Stripe**, a secure and trusted payment platform used by thousands of organisations worldwide (including the NHS and the British Red Cross).

- **Shop purchases** — customers complete their purchase entirely online. They enter their delivery address and pay by card. Shipping is restricted to the **UK and France** only. Every order is recorded and visible in the admin panel with the customer's full details.
- **Donations** — visitors can choose a preset amount (£5, £10, £25, £50) or enter their own. The donation is processed instantly and securely. Every donation is logged so the centre can keep track of its fundraising.
- All transactions are confirmed automatically — no manual work needed from staff.

---

## Admin Panel

A private dashboard accessible only to the centre's staff, protected by a login and password. Nothing here is visible to the public.

| Section | What staff can do |
|---------|-------------------|
| **Dashboard** | A live overview of all recent orders — customer name, email address, delivery address, item purchased, amount paid and current status. Everything in one place. |
| **Shop** | Add new products, update prices, change photos, edit descriptions, or remove items that are no longer available. No technical knowledge required. |
| **News** | Write new articles, edit existing ones, and choose exactly when to publish them. Unpublished drafts are saved but invisible to the public until ready. |
| **Trips** | Create and manage battlefield tour listings — title, description, date and maximum number of participants. |
| **Events** | Add special events at the centre — exhibitions, commemorations, school visits — with date, location and description. |
| **Volunteers** | See every volunteer application with the person's name, contact details and availability. Approve or reject each one with a single click. |
| **Settings** | Update the organisation's name and logo displayed across the site. |
